Hajj Guide

The five days of Hajj, step by step, as performed by most Pakistani pilgrims (Hajj Tamattu: Umrah first, then Hajj). Our scholars and group leaders guide every step on the ground.

Types of Hajj

  • Tamattu: Umrah in the months of Hajj, leave Ihram, then enter Ihram again for Hajj on 8 Dhul Hijjah. Sacrifice required. Most common for pilgrims from Pakistan.
  • Qiran: Umrah and Hajj in one Ihram. Sacrifice required.
  • Ifrad: Hajj only. No sacrifice required.

8 Dhul Hijjah: Yawm at-Tarwiyah

  • Enter Ihram for Hajj from your Makkah residence, recite the Talbiyah and travel to Mina.
  • Pray Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and Fajr in Mina (shortened, not combined).

9 Dhul Hijjah: Day of Arafat

  • After sunrise move to Arafat. Standing (wuquf) at Arafat is the heart of Hajj: dua, dhikr, Quran and repentance until sunset.
  • After sunset travel to Muzdalifah, pray Maghrib and Isha together, collect pebbles and rest overnight under the sky.

10 Dhul Hijjah: Eid al-Adha

  • Pray Fajr in Muzdalifah, then go to Mina and stone the large Jamarah (Jamarat al-Aqabah) with seven pebbles.
  • Sacrifice (arranged through the official coupon system), then shave or trim. Change into normal clothes.
  • Go to Makkah for Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i, then return to Mina.

11 to 13 Dhul Hijjah: Days of Tashreeq

  • Stone all three Jamarat each day after Zuhr, small, middle then large, seven pebbles each.
  • Stay in Mina overnight. Leaving on 12 Dhul Hijjah before sunset is allowed; staying to 13 is better.

Farewell

  • Tawaf al-Wada (farewell Tawaf) is the last act before leaving Makkah.

Health and heat advice

  • Drink water constantly, use an umbrella and sunscreen, rest between rituals, and walk with your group.
  • Keep your camp number card, group leader's number and hotel card on you at all times.
  • Elderly pilgrims can delegate stoning to a companion; wheelchair services are available on request.
