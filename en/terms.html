Payments and Terms

How payments work at Al Multazam Travels. Prices are per person in PKR and depend on room sharing. Every price is confirmed in a written quote, which we send within 24 hours of your inquiry.

Advance: 30% of the package price confirms your seats and hotel.

Balance: the remaining amount is due 15 days before departure.

How to pay: by bank transfer or in person at our Lahore office (3-L Commercial, DHA Phase 8). We issue a receipt for every payment. You can check your booking status on the Track Booking page.

Cancellation: cancellation charges follow the airline and hotel policies that apply to your booking. Visa fees are non-refundable once the visa is issued.

Visa refusal: refusals are rare. If a visa is refused, the package amount minus the visa fee is refunded or moved to a later date.
