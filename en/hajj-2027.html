Hajj 2027

Hajj 2027 (Dhul Hijjah 1448) follows the Ministry of Religious Affairs Hajj Policy and Plan 2027-2030. Register your interest with us and we will guide you through the official process step by step.

How Hajj 2027 booking works

  • Two schemes. Under the policy, 60% of seats are government scheme and 40% private scheme. Reports in September 2026 said 71,684 seats were allocated to the private scheme.
  • Private packages. Sixteen basic private packages were reported for 2027, from about Rs 1.4 million to Rs 3.75 million, from short-stay to five-star.
  • Booking and payment are official. Private bookings go through the Ministry's Pak Hajj app and portal, and each pilgrim receives a Unique Hajj Identification Number. Under the policy, money is paid into a government account, not to an operator.
  • Please be careful. Do not pay Hajj money to any person or company directly. Pay only through the official portal, and keep the receipt.
  • Dates. News reports gave 20 November 2026 as the private scheme selection and payment deadline. Please check the official portal for the current position before you apply.

Sources: Ministry of Religious Affairs, Hajj Policy and Plan 2027-2030; ARY News and Pakistan Connect, September 2026. Rules and dates can change.

Who can go

  • A Pakistani national with a valid Pakistani passport. The minimum validity and age limits are notified each season following Saudi instructions.
  • A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner. Terminally ill patients are not permitted.
  • Women: under the 2023 Council of Islamic Ideology decision, a mahram is not required if an affidavit is submitted (permission of parents or husband, travelling in a group of reliable women, no threat to dignity or safety).
  • Persons with special needs must travel with a designated attendant.
  • People on the Exit Control List or under a court order barring travel abroad cannot go.
  • Hujjaj Muhafiz scheme: a non-refundable Rs 1,000 contribution per pilgrim, with compensation for death in Saudi Arabia and for emergency evacuation due to illness.

What to expect

  • Makkah hotel near the Haram before and after the Hajj days.
  • Azizia residence during the Hajj days, Mina camp with meals, Arafat and Muzdalifah transport.
  • Madinah stay with Ziyarat of Masjid an-Nabawi, Quba, Uhud and Baqi.
  • Scholar guidance throughout, pre-Hajj training sessions in Lahore, printed and WhatsApp guides.
  • Group leaders from the Naeem family on every departure.

Documents needed

  • Scanned colour copy of a valid Pakistani passport. The required validity is confirmed when you register.
  • Passport-size photo with a white or blue background.
  • Scanned colour copy of CNIC / NICOP, and of your next of kin with a mobile number.
  • Your blood group (a blood report is not required).
  • Choice of Hajj package: 14 days or 20 days.
  • Vaccination records as required by the Saudi guidelines for the season.
  • Payment receipt from the official Pak Hajj portal.
