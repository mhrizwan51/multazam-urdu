Umrah Guide

A step-by-step guide for first-time pilgrims. Every Al Multazam group also receives a training session in Lahore before departure and a printed copy of this guide.

1. Before departure

  • Make a sincere intention (niyyah), settle debts, seek forgiveness from family and friends.
  • Learn the rituals and the duas; attend our pre-departure Umrah training.
  • Pack Ihram, medicines with prescriptions, comfortable slippers and copies of all documents.

2. Ihram at the Miqat

  • On flights from Pakistan, assume Ihram before boarding or when the crew announces the Miqat (Yalamlam or Qarn al-Manazil).
  • Men: two pieces of unstitched white cloth. Women: normal modest clothing; the face is not covered with a niqab in Ihram.
  • Pray two rakats, make the intention for Umrah and recite the Talbiyah: Labbayk Allahumma labbayk.
  • Avoid perfume, cutting hair or nails, and arguments while in Ihram.

3. Tawaf

  • Enter Masjid al-Haram with the right foot and the dua for entering the mosque.
  • Seven circuits of the Kaaba starting at the Black Stone, counter-clockwise, Kaaba on your left.
  • Between Rukn Yamani and the Black Stone recite: Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah wa fil-akhirati hasanah wa qina adhab an-nar.
  • Pray two rakats behind Maqam Ibrahim and drink Zamzam.

4. Sa'i

  • Seven laps between Safa and Marwah, beginning at Safa and ending at Marwah.
  • Men walk briskly between the green lights; women walk normally.

5. Halq or Taqsir

  • Men shave the head (preferred) or trim all over; women cut a fingertip's length of hair.
  • Your Umrah is complete and the restrictions of Ihram end. Alhamdulillah.

Ziyarat

  • Madinah: Masjid an-Nabawi and the Rawdah (permit via the Nusuk app, booked by our team), Jannat al-Baqi, Masjid Quba, Mount Uhud, Masjid al-Qiblatayn.
  • Makkah: Jabal al-Noor (Cave of Hira), Jabal Thawr, Arafat, Mina, Muzdalifah, Masjid Aisha (Tan'im) for a second Umrah.

Essential duas

  • Talbiyah: Labbayk Allahumma labbayk, labbayka la sharika laka labbayk, innal hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.
  • On seeing the Kaaba: Allahumma zid hadhal-bayta tashrifan wa ta'ziman wa takriman wa mahabah.
  • Entering Masjid an-Nabawi: Allahumma iftah li abwaba rahmatik.
  • At Safa and Marwah: Innas-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah.

Packing checklist

  • Passport, visa printout, tickets, hotel vouchers, CNIC copy, vaccination record.
  • Two sets of Ihram, Ihram belt, slippers, unscented soap, small bag for shoes.
  • Medicines with prescription, power bank, Saudi SIM or eSIM, prayer mat, prayer beads.
  • Pakistani rupees for the airport, Saudi riyals in small notes, a debit card.
  • Umbrella or cap, water bottle, light cotton clothing, warm layer for Madinah winters.
