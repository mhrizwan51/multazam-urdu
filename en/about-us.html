About Us

Al-Multazam Travels (Pvt) Ltd has served pilgrims since 1981 from its head office in Lahore. The company is run by the Naeem family and offers Umrah and Hajj packages, worldwide ticketing, visa services and group tours to clients in Pakistan and to the Pakistani community in the UK, USA, Canada and Europe.

Our name

The Multazam is the blessed wall between the Black Stone and the door of the Kaaba, where pilgrims press their chest and hands and their duas are accepted. Our aim is to bring every client to that spot with ease and peace of mind.

Our promise

  • Honest pricing: hotel names and distances in every quote, receipts for every payment.
  • Experience: 45 years, three generations, thousands of pilgrims.
  • Presence: group leaders travelling with every group, partners in Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah.
  • Guidance: training before departure and support at every step until you are home.
