Hajj 2027

Hajj 2027 (Dhul Hijjah 1448) registration is open. Packages are announced after the Ministry of Religious Affairs publishes the Hajj Policy 2027. Register your interest now to be first in line when seats open.

What to expect

  • Makkah hotel near the Haram before and after the Hajj days.
  • Azizia residence during the Hajj days, Mina camp with meals, Arafat and Muzdalifah transport.
  • Madinah stay with Ziyarat of Masjid an-Nabawi, Quba, Uhud and Baqi.
  • Scholar guidance throughout, pre-Hajj training sessions in Lahore, printed and WhatsApp guides.
  • Group leaders from the Naeem family on every departure.

Documents needed

  • Passport valid for at least six months after return.
  • CNIC copy and recent photographs with white background.
  • Vaccination certificate (meningococcal ACYW and polio).
  • Advance payment receipt from a bank channel.
