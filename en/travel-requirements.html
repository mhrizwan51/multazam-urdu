What Pakistani pilgrims need before travelling to Saudi Arabia for Umrah or Hajj. Rules change; we confirm the current requirements at the time of your booking.

Umrah visa for Pakistani citizens

  • Issued through a Ministry-approved operator such as Al Multazam; pilgrims cannot apply directly on Nusuk.
  • Passport valid for at least six months; clear scanned copy and a white-background photo.
  • Visa fee about SAR 300 plus mandatory insurance; usually valid for entry within 30 days and a stay of up to 90 days.
  • Processing 3 to 5 working days once documents are complete.

Health requirements

  • Meningococcal ACYW vaccine at least 10 days before travel; certificate valid for 3 to 5 years depending on the vaccine.
  • Polio vaccination certificate (bOPV or IPV) for all travellers from Pakistan.
  • Carry your digital vaccination record; airport staff may ask for it at check-in.
  • Bring regular medicines with the prescription; pharmacies in Makkah and Madinah stock most common medicines.

Nusuk app and permits

  • Rawdah (Riyad ul Jannah) visits and Umrah permits are issued through the Nusuk app; our team books Rawdah slots for every pilgrim in the group.
  • Hajj pilgrims receive a Nusuk card; keep it with you at all times during the Hajj days.

Money

  • Currency: Saudi riyal (SAR). Exchange in Pakistan or at the hotel; cards work in most shops and restaurants.
  • Keep small notes for taxis, barbers and shopping near the Haram.

Mobile and internet

  • Saudi SIMs (STC, Mobily, Zain) are sold at Jeddah and Madinah airports; an eSIM can be bought before departure.
  • Every group has a WhatsApp group with the group leader for daily announcements.

Weather and clothing

  • Makkah is hot most of the year; Madinah is cooler in winter nights. Ramadan 2027 falls in February to March.
  • Light cotton clothing, comfortable slippers, an umbrella or cap, and one warm layer for Madinah.

Customs and etiquette

  • Respect prayer times, queue patiently at the Haram gates, and keep the Haram clean.
  • Photography inside the mosques is allowed only without disturbing worshippers; drones are not allowed.
