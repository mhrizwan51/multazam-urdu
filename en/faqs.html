FAQs

How far is the hotel from the Haram?

Every package lists the hotel name and its distance in metres, plus the shuttle arrangement for hotels beyond walking distance.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. An advance confirms your seat and hotel, and the balance is due before visa issuance. Ask about our Umrah savings plan for a departure six to twelve months away.

Do you arrange Umrah for women travelling without a mahram?

Yes, within current Saudi rules, with group leaders on every departure and family-friendly room allocation.

Can I extend my stay?

Yes, subject to visa validity (up to 90 days) and airline change fees.

What if my visa is refused?

Refusals are rare. The package amount minus the visa fee is refunded or moved to a later date.

Do you provide wheelchairs for elderly pilgrims?

Yes, on request, at both airports and inside the Haram; our group leaders arrange it.

Is the Rawdah visit included?

We book Rawdah permits on the Nusuk app for every pilgrim in the group.

Which airlines do you use?

Saudia, PIA and other carriers depending on the dates and promo fares. Saudia promo fares are announced on our Facebook page.

Can I choose my own hotel or dates?

Yes, custom packages for families and groups are available; send your preferences on WhatsApp.

Do you serve pilgrims living abroad?

Yes. We arrange Umrah and Hajj for Pakistanis in the UK, USA, Canada and Europe, including meeting family members in Makkah.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp 0300 1853111 or use the inquiry form on the Contact page; written quotes within 24 hours.
